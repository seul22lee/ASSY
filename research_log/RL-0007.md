# RL-0007 — Renderer readability and coverage audit

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Contract touched:** none. Renderer and audit only (`assy/conceptrender.py`, `assy/conceptcoverage.py`)

## Summary

The Stage 04 sheet was drawing spatial placement well and mechanism behaviour not
at all. Six visual layers were added — true swept shapes, motion glyphs, interface
glyphs, constraint status colouring, annotation callouts and issue markers — using
only fields the blueprint already carried. A two-way coverage audit was added so
the picture cannot drift away from the representation in either direction.

## Trigger

A review question: does the renderer expose the mechanical information that
already exists? The audit that followed was quantitative, not impressionistic.

## Evidence

The renderer read **6 of the blueprint's 20 keys**. Within those it read a handful
of sub-fields, and it never read `motion_kind` at all — so a rotating crank and a
translating platform rendered as identical axis-aligned boxes.

`spatial_constraints` — 15 typed entries naming the screw/nut engagement, the
hinge joint, the latch contact, the guide span and the thrust station — was used
**only to print a count in the legend**. `annotations` (14) and `issues` (15) were
not drawn at all.

Classification of the nine mechanical facts asked about: six were already in the
blueprint and simply not drawn; three (motion transformation, functional chain,
load path) existed in Stage 02/03 but were not forwarded. Only one thing was
genuinely missing from the representation: the held/released state pair is prose
(`holding_principle`, `state_relations`), so a two-state diagram is not
constructible.

## Root cause

Not a representational gap. The blueprint had grown considerably across RL-0004
to RL-0006 while the renderer was written against its earliest shape and never
revisited. There was no mechanism that would notice.

## Design decision

Implement A–F (renderer-only) and defer G and H (forwarding for functional chain
and load path), on the principle that the cycle's goal was readability of the
existing representation rather than expansion of it.

Behaviour and review were given **their own panels** rather than overlaid on the
spatial views. The first attempt overlaid everything and the sheet became
unreadable — each layer was individually correct and the composite was useless.

The **coverage audit** was added to prevent recurrence, and it is deliberately
bidirectional. One direction stops the picture asserting engineering the contracts
never stated — the L1 failure mode in visual form. The other stops a field being
silently ignored, which to a reviewer looks exactly like a field the architecture
does not carry. Ten fields are drawn; ten are excluded with stated reasons, mostly
because they are prose restatements of structured fields already drawn, or because
drawing them would double-report a conflict and inflate apparent severity.

Rejected: a generic fallback mark for unrecognised relation kinds. A generic mark
makes two different mechanical demands look identical, so a test asserts every
`SpatialRelationKind` has its own mark and that no two marks collide.

## Stage ownership

Nothing moved. Every layer reads existing blueprint fields; no schema changed. The
registries live in `conceptcoverage`, separate from the drawing code, so the audit
cannot be quietly bypassed by editing the renderer.

## Generalization

The glyph vocabulary is keyed on enum values, not on element names, so a new
mechanism family inherits it. Completeness is enforced rather than assumed: tests
assert every relation kind, every constraint status and every motion kind has a
declared glyph, so adding one upstream fails the suite until the renderer is
extended.

## Validation performed

- Coverage on both benchmarks: **10 visualized, 10 explicitly excluded, 0
  unaccounted**; no orphan glyphs, no undeclared glyphs, no double declarations.
- BM-001's `shared_axis` violation now renders as a red ✗ on the line between
  `closure_member` and `opening_interface` — the RL-0005 finding is visible on the
  drawing rather than only in JSON.
- Rendering remains byte-identical across runs; `render_coverage.json` ships beside
  the image.
- 15 new tests; **198 deterministic tests pass**.

## Freeze status

Renderer not frozen — see below. The Stage 02–04 contracts are untouched and
retain the status recorded in RL-0006.

## Remaining issues

- Labels still overprint where several pieces occupy one zone; the stagger helps
  but does not solve it.
- The isometric panel is the least legible of the ten and carries no glyph layers.
- Functional chain and load path remain undrawn (G and H, deferred by decision).
- The held/released state pair remains unrepresentable; recorded as the one
  genuine gap rather than worked around.

## Next validation target

Either forward the chain and load-path fields (G, H) and add their two panels, or
resolve the BM-001 `shared_axis` violation by constraint-driven placement, which
RL-0006 identified as the larger open item.
