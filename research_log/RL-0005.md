# RL-0005 — Stage 04 visualization and visual review

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Contract touched:** `PlacedPiece`, `RegionPlacement.houses`, `assy/conceptrender.py`, `visual_review.json`

## Summary

Stage 04 now emits three artifacts: the blueprint (`output.json`), a rendered
block layout (`concept_layout.png`), and a structured visual review
(`visual_review.json`). The renderer turns qualitative zones into drawable slots
under a convention it owns; the images are a review artifact and never an
authority.

## Trigger

The stage was named "Concept Visualization" and produced no visualization. The
gap between the name and the output was the trigger; the deeper motive was that
structured checks and pictures fail in different ways.

## Evidence

This was the first revision where **rendering produced evidence a structured
check had not**. Four findings, three of them new:

1. **The shell was being relocated by an obligation it reacts.** `housing` reacts
   several obligations; the first encountered moved it to `external` on BM-001 and
   `end` on BM-002. Invisible in JSON, obvious the moment a box was drawn.
2. **The lid was drawn buried inside the housing.** A `moving_boundary` element is
   the product's own wall, not something inside the storage volume.
3. **BM-001's hinge sits on the opposite side of the box from the lid it carries.**
   The blueprint records `closure_member guidance ← opening_interface` and even a
   `parallel` placement, but zone assignment is independent: the obligation put
   the hinge in `FLANKING` while its subject is `BOUNDARY`.
4. **BM-002's payload cannot reach the platform.** The payload region renders
   outside the shell, the platform is in the core, and the only boundary crossings
   are the crank and the service cover. Each fact was individually reasonable in
   JSON; the contradiction appears only when they are placed in one frame.

## Root cause

Two distinct causes.

For (1) and (2): the obligation override was applied at the wrong granularity —
to *regions* rather than to the *elements* that carry the constraint. The first
attempted fix for (2) moved the whole storage volume to the boundary, which the
next render exposed immediately; it now applies per piece.

For (3) and (4): the blueprint has no notion of **adjacency** or of an **access
path**. Zones express distance from the primary axis, so nothing requires a
reactor to be co-located with what it reacts, and nothing asks whether an external
agent can reach an internal target.

Separately, the blueprint was **not self-contained**: it placed regions but
carried no piece inventory and no region→element mapping, so a renderer could draw
boxes but not what was inside them.

## Design decision

Add `PlacedPiece` and `RegionPlacement.houses` so the blueprint stands alone — a
consumer must not re-join with Stage 03 to know what is being arranged.

The layout convention belongs to the **renderer**, not the blueprint. Zones map to
slots in a unit envelope; those extents are nominal drawing units carrying no
engineering meaning, and no downstream stage may read them. This keeps the
blueprint dimension-free while still permitting a picture.

The visual review is recorded as an artifact of a *review*, marked as a model
visual review with no authority, each finding tagged with the views it is visible
in and whether it was new in the visualization. Findings (3) and (4) were
**reported, not fixed** — they need a richer contract, and patching the renderer
to make the pictures look right would have destroyed the evidence.

## Stage ownership

| Field | Owner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `placed_pieces`, `houses` | Stage 04 | it is the stage arranging them |
| layout convention, slot extents | renderer | a drawing convention is not engineering content |
| `visual_review.json` | review artifact | no authority; `output.json` remains authoritative |

## Generalization

The renderer reads only the blueprint. A render failure yields no image rather
than a failed stage, and a test asserts a structurally invalid blueprint produces
zero images without raising.

## Validation performed

- Both benchmarks rendered: 7 panels — isometric, front, side, top, cutaway, and
  regions-by-zone in two projections.
- **Byte-identical rendering across repeated runs**, asserted by SHA-256.
- 5 new tests; **160 deterministic tests pass**.

## Freeze status

Not frozen. The layouts are organisationally complete but not spatially coherent:
BM-002 has no load path for its payload and BM-001's hinge does not touch its
closure.

## Remaining issues

Four defects reported and left open, with ownership assigned:

| Defect | Owner |
|---|---|
| reactor not required to be adjacent to what it reacts | Stage 04 |
| `END` does not distinguish the two ends of an axis | Stage 04 |
| `BOUNDARY` carries no face identity | Stage 04 |
| no access path connecting an external agent to an internal target | Stage 03 |
| guidance against an eccentric load needs opposed reaction | Stage 02 |

## Next validation target

Repair the contracts these defects expose, as general invariants rather than as
fixes fitted to the two rendered examples.
