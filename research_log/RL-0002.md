# RL-0002 — Stage 02 architecture completeness

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Contract touched:** `SupportObligation`, `ArchitecturalInterface`, `ArchitecturalFunction`, candidate scoring

## Summary

Stage 02's architecture content was made machine-consumable. Support obligations
and interfaces became typed objects naming the elements involved, the golden
"functional chain" was separated from the element chain it had been conflated
with, and quantitative bounds were linked to the architecture they constrain.

## Trigger

A comparison of the actual Stage 02 output against `BM-001_GOLDEN_STAGE_OUTPUTS.md`
and `BM-002_GOLDEN_STAGE_OUTPUTS.md`, treating the golden files as references for
reasoning *depth* rather than as templates.

## Evidence

| | BM-001 | BM-002 |
|---|---|---|
| Golden functional-chain items covered | 3 / 9 | 4 / 10 |
| Typed support obligations | 1 (prose) | 2 (prose) |
| Typed interfaces | 1 (prose) | 3 (prose) |
| Bound traceability | — | none |

The decisive evidence was downstream: `s03_product.py` ignored
`support_obligations`, `interfaces`, `load_path`, `spatial_implications` and
`motion_envelopes` **entirely**, and instead re-read requirement prose
(`"enclos" in text`) and hardcoded its own load paths. It was not being lazy — it
could not consume a sentence like "the travelling member must be guided against
rotation" without parsing English.

## Root cause

The architecture content existed, but as `list[str]`. Nothing expressed *which
element* bore *which kind* of obligation, or *which two elements* an interface
joined. The field named `functional_chain` compounded this by holding an element
sequence, not the sequence of functions the golden documents mean by that term.

## Design decision

Two enums (`ObligationKind` with 9 values, `InterfaceKind` with 8), three models,
three new `MechanismRole` values (`SUPPORT`, `LIMIT`, `RELEASE`), and the split of
`functional_chain` into `functions` and `element_chain`. Prose survives only in a
`why` field, as explanation.

A second decision was forced mid-work. Enriching the catalogue with the missing
conceptual elements (bearings, thrust supports, travel stops, opening interfaces)
raised element counts, and because the score was `-0.12 × part_count`, BM-002
flipped to `flexible_tension_drive` — a family whose own holding principle reads
"tension is lost when input is released". A platform lift would have dropped its
payload.

Rejected: tuning the weights until the expected family won. That would have been
benchmark optimisation. Instead the gap was made **structural**: a family may
declare `Fn(function=…, performed_by=[])`, and the scorer penalises functions the
architecture cannot itself perform. `screw_drive` then wins on merit because it
holds inherently, and the rejection text says so. `part_count` became a derived
tiebreaker — the hand-set numbers had already drifted from the role lists.

## Stage ownership

| Field | Owner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `ObligationKind` / `InterfaceKind` tables | knowledge base | "a nut needs anti-rotation" is mechanism knowledge |
| `support_obligations`, `interfaces`, `functions` | Stage 02 | conceptual architecture is its product |
| `constrained_by` | Stage 02 | it holds the requirement records the bounds came from |

Explicitly *not* Stage 02: obligation **ownership**. Which piece reacts an
obligation is a product decision and belongs downstream.

## Generalization

Every obligation and interface names declared elements only, and a test asserts
this. No dimension may appear in any obligation, interface or function — asserted
by regex over the emitted text. The unassigned-function rule is stated over the
data, not over a family list, so a new family inherits it.

## Validation performed

- Golden functional-chain coverage rose to **9/9 (BM-001)** and **10/10 (BM-002)**.
- Typed obligations 1→6 and 2→8; typed interfaces 1→4 and 3→7.
- BM-002 bounds `REQ-002` (80–100 mm, `bounded`) and `REQ-003` (~1 kg,
  `approximate`) now reach the architecture via `constrained_by`.
- 14 new tests; **90 deterministic tests pass**. Source-text invariance re-proven
  over the new typed content, not just the old fields.

## Freeze status

**Provisionally frozen for BM-001 and BM-002.** The blocking question — can
Stage 03 organise a product without rereading the benchmark — is answered yes.

## Remaining issues

- `load_path`, `spatial_implications` and `motion_envelopes` remain `list[str]`.
  The first is an element sequence that could be typed cheaply; the latter two are
  genuinely qualitative at this level.
- BM-001 selects `friction_detent_catch` over `compliant_catch` on a 1.45 vs 1.43
  margin — effectively a tie. Within the golden's terms, which accept several
  principles, but the selection is not strongly evidenced.
- Stage 03 does not yet consume any of the new fields. Supplying them and using
  them are separate revisions.

## Next validation target

Whether Stage 03 can be made a strict consumer of the selected candidate, and
whether doing so removes its prose parsing and hardcoded product assumptions.
