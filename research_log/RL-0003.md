# RL-0003 — Stage 03 as a strict consumer

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Contract touched:** `ProductPiece`, `ObligationOwnership`, `ProductInterface`, `PlacementRelation`, `AssemblyStep`, `LoadPathOwnership`, `FunctionalPart.moving`

## Summary

Stage 03 was rewritten to organise the selected Stage 02 candidate into a product
without rereading anything upstream of it. It now emits manufactured pieces, an
owner for every support obligation, piece-level interfaces, qualitative
placements, an assembly order and load-path ownership by region.

## Trigger

An audit of what Stage 03 actually read before changing it.

## Evidence

Stage 03 consumed exactly two things from Stage 02: `selected.parts` (roles →
regions) and `selected.risks`. Everything else it emitted was either parsed from
requirement prose or hardcoded:

- `"service" in text`, `"enclos" in text` at `s03_product.py:57-59`
- `housing_strategy`, `assembly_strategy`, `service_strategy`,
  `protection_strategy`, `manufacturing_intent`, `proportions` — string literals
- `load_paths` asserted `"payload → output element → guidance → structural shell
  → base"` for **BM-001, which has no payload**, and `proportions` asserted
  "output travel along the tall axis" for a storage box

The stage produced nearly the same product regardless of its input.

## Root cause

Two defects, one in each direction. Upstream, the typed architecture content from
RL-0002 was not being read. Downstream, the schema could not have expressed the
answer even if it had been: `ProductArchitecture` was `regions[] + six free-text
strings + load_paths: list[str]`, with no representation for a manufactured
piece, an obligation owner, a placement, an assembly step or traceability.

Hardcoding was the only way to fill fields that had no derivable content, so the
literals were a symptom of the schema, not of carelessness.

## Design decision

Add the six models above; derive every field from the selected candidate's typed
content. Where the architecture leaves a choice open, record it as unresolved
rather than deciding it — `manufacturing_intent` is now **empty**, because process
is a design freedom and Stage 03 was previously asserting "additive-friendly" as
though it were a conclusion.

Piece-to-element mapping is 1:1. Integrating several conceptual elements into one
manufactured piece (a latch moulded into a lid) is a *decision*, so it is recorded
as unresolved rather than proposed. This is honest but means Stage 03 does not yet
reduce part count.

Three corrections were made during the work, each because the first rule was
wrong in a way visible in the output: an applied-load region was being named
`payload_region` even when the load was a disturbance; a user-access region was
missing whenever the release element carried a non-`RELEASE` role; and a service
cover was being invented beside a closure that already opens the product.

## Stage ownership

| Field | Owner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `ProductPiece`, `AssemblyStep`, `ObligationOwnership` | Stage 03 | manufactured identity and ownership are product decisions |
| `FunctionalPart.moving` | **Stage 02** | whether an element moves is mechanism knowledge |
| `unresolved_decisions` | Stage 03 | it must not close what the architecture left open |

## Generalization

Enclosure is now derived from structure — a shell element plus a boundary-crossing
interface — not from the word "enclosure" appearing in a requirement. A test
deletes the substrings `enclos` and `service` from every requirement statement and
asserts the service cover still appears.

## Contract deficiency reported

`FunctionalPart` had no `moving` flag. The moving/stationary distinction existed
**only** inside the prose `rationale` string `"moving element in the … chain"`.
Stage 03 cannot derive swept volumes without parsing that. Fixed at the source in
Stage 02 rather than parsed around — this is the one upstream change this revision
made, and it is recorded here because a stage reaching backwards for information
is exactly the coupling RL-0001 removed.

Two further gaps were reported and **not** compensated for: engineering role tags
are still absent from `FunctionalPart`, so Stage 05 re-queries the catalogue by
`selected_id`; and `load_path` / `spatial_implications` remain prose.

## Validation performed

- Golden region-kind coverage **7/7 (BM-001)** and **8/8 (BM-002)**.
- Obligations owned 6/6 and 8/8; placements 7 and 10; assembly steps 5 and 10.
- Invariance under four mutations (blank text, decoy text, scrambled statements,
  relabelled identity) — full fingerprint bit-identical.
- Consumption of only the selected candidate proven two ways: no element of any
  rejected candidate appears anywhere, and forcing a different `selected_id`
  changes the fingerprint.
- 35 new tests; **125 deterministic tests pass**.

## Freeze status

**Provisionally frozen.** Stage 04 can review this spatially and Stage 05 can
synthesise from it without rereading the benchmark.

## Remaining issues

- `ASSEMBLY_RANK` is a fixed piece-kind ordering, not a topological sort of the
  obligation graph. It satisfies every supports-before-supported constraint in
  both benchmarks (asserted by test), but a family whose obligations contradict
  that ranking would produce a wrong order silently.
- `housing_strategy` and `protection_strategy` are still selected from two
  phrasings each — derived from structure now, but not themselves structured.
