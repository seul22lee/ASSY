# RL-0004 — Stage 04 spatial concept analysis

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Contract touched:** `ConceptVisualization` (reference frame, region placements, swept volumes, interference candidates, access routes, typed issues)

## Summary

Stage 04 was redefined from "emit a textual spatial hypothesis" to "arrange the
product organisation in space and report where it contradicts itself". It is the
first stage that places regions relative to one another, and therefore the first
that can notice two of them wanting the same space.

## Trigger

The question was deliberately asked before looking at the existing implementation:
*what must Stage 04 produce so that Stage 05 can synthesise geometry without
independently solving the product layout problem?*

## Evidence

The prior implementation emitted four hardcoded `spatial_hypotheses` strings and
three `review_concerns` strings, identical for every product.

The audit of Stage 03's output found it already supported five of the six
capabilities Stage 04 needs — region organisation, moving/stationary assignment,
accessibility regions, interference derivation, qualitative layout. Exactly one
was blocked: **per-element motion kind**. Stage 03 carried `moving: bool` but never
*how*. Stage 02's `motions` list declares a single aggregate relation for the whole
chain (`input_member → travelling_member`), so `transmission_shaft` and
`threaded_member` were moving with no motion information at all.

## Root cause

`FunctionalPart` carried no engineering role tags. The per-element motion data
(`rotating`, `translating`, `moving_boundary`) lived in the catalogue's
`RoleTemplate.roles` and was dropped at the Stage 02 boundary — which is why
`s05_engineering.py:111` re-queries the knowledge base by `selected_id`, reaching
around Stage 03 to get it. Stage 04 would have had to do the same.

## Design decision

Emit a typed blueprint rather than prose: reference frame, a zone per region,
swept volumes classified by motion, interference candidates, access routes, and a
structured issue list. The output stays `authoritative = False` permanently, but
**non-authoritative was decoupled from unstructured** — an issue Stage 05 must act
on is useless as a paragraph.

Two derivation choices are worth recording. Translation dominates rotation when
choosing the frame, so a product whose output translates is organised along that
travel whatever else spins inside it. And elements joined by a **declared
interface are designed to meet**, so a threaded pair is marked `addressed` rather
than reported as a collision — without that rule the list was 18 mostly-spurious
entries for BM-002, and a noisy issue list is worse than none because nothing in
it can be acted on.

The upstream fix was `FunctionalPart.engineering_roles`, populated from the
catalogue's existing `RoleTemplate.roles` and forwarded onto `ProductPiece`.

## Stage ownership

| Field | Owner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `engineering_roles` | Stage 02 | what an element is for is mechanism knowledge |
| reference frame, placements, swept volumes | Stage 04 | arrangement is its question |
| interference candidates, issues | Stage 04 | only a placement can contradict itself |
| `product_advisories` | pass-through | Stage 03 gaps are reported, never resolved here |

## Generalization

Swept-volume classification keys on role tags, not element names, so a new family
classifies automatically. A structural test asserts `spec.`, `source_text` and
`r.statement` cannot appear in the Stage 04 source — the stage does not read
`RequirementSpec` at all.

## Validation performed

- BM-001: 9 regions placed, 2 swept volumes, 7 interference candidates (2
  unresolved), 2 issues. BM-002: 13 regions, 4 swept, 17 candidates (10
  unresolved), 14 issues. The golden BM-002 section expects ~10 issues of
  comparable kind, two of which — service access blocked by the crank sweep, and
  payload-face obstruction — were independently detected.
- Full fingerprint invariant under blank text, a flat-open-tray decoy, scrambled
  statements and a relabelled identity.
- 30 new tests; **155 deterministic tests pass**.

## Bug found by the tests

`closure_member` owns an `alignment` obligation, which mapped to zone `CORE` and
was overriding its own externally-reachable access region — placing a surface the
user must reach *inside* the product. An obligation may now refine only an
internal region's zone.

## Freeze status

Not frozen. The blueprint is structurally complete but had not yet been rendered,
and rendering is what tests whether an arrangement is coherent rather than merely
well-typed.

## Remaining issues

- Interference detection is containment-based ("same side of the boundary, no
  declared relationship"). It over-reports for products with many co-located
  static regions and cannot detect a genuine collision between two regions that
  *do* have an interface but are badly proportioned.
- Zones are a six-value vocabulary with no notion of opposite sides, so "guides on
  opposite sides of the screw" reduces to both being `FLANKING`.
- `image_refs` is empty: no human has visually reviewed either layout.

## Next validation target

Render the blueprint and review the images. A structured check and a picture fail
in different ways; the picture is the test of whether the arrangement is
physically sensible.
